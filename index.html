How it
works

Design is a way to solve problems through visual and user experience. A designer's job is to transform complex ideas and processes into clear, accessible, and functional digital solutions.

01.

Briefing

Defining the project goals, business objectives and expectations that the design should address.

02.

Research

Study the product, audience, and context of use to identify problems and opportunities.

03.

Concept

Formation of an idea and a general direction that determines the logic and nature of the future solution.

04.

Design

Creating a structure, interface, and visual language that provides a clear and consistent user experience.

05.

The Finale

Preparation of design materials for the correct implementation of the product and file transfer.
